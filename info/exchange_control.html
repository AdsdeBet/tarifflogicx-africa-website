Exchange control (SARB)

APN advance payments, export proceeds repatriation, and BoP codes

Exchange control is a separate regulatory track from customs and tax — governed by the Currency and Exchanges Act of 1933 and the 1961 Exchange Control Regulations, administered by the Financial Surveillance Department (FinSurv) of the South African Reserve Bank (SARB), not SARS.
