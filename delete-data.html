Operator: Raven & Rose Digital Products, South Africa

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@tarifflogicxafrica.co.za from the same context you use the app (mention your device/platform — Android) with the subject line "Data deletion request."
  2. Tell us which data you want deleted — see the table below for what we hold. If you want everything deleted, just say so.
  3. We will confirm and action your request within 30 days, as required under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Most data the app stores is already deletable by you directly, with no request needed — see the table below for what's self-service versus what requires contacting us.
