How it works

From product to landed cost in a few taps — no account, no spreadsheet.

Find the HS code

Search by product name, type the code directly, scan a barcode, or describe the product in plain language and let AI Classify suggest it — with confidence levels and reasoning.

Check duty, VAT & permits

Every result shows the General duty rate plus preferential rates (SACU, SADC, AfCFTA, EU/UK EPA, EFTA, MERCOSUR), VAT treatment, and whether a permit is required — and from which authority.

Calculate the landed cost

Enter the goods value, freight, and insurance and get the real Added Tax Value (ATV) and total landed cost — using SARS' actual formula, not a simplified estimate.

Browse the reference library

Not sure where to start? Browse all 98 HS chapters from Home, or check Important Information for HazChem, Incoterms® 2020, trade agreements, permits by authority, and more.
